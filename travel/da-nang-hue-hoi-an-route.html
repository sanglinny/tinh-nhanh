Da Nang – Hue – Hoi An: how to get between them in 2026

Vietnam travelUpdated 12/11/20265 min readEdited by Tính Nhanh

Fly into Da Nang, go north to Hue by train along the Hai Van coast (about 2.5–4 hours) or by car through the Hai Van Tunnel (about 2–2.5 hours), then come back south to Hoi An, about 45–60 minutes by car from Da Nang airport. There is no train to Hoi An: rail travellers change to a car or bus in Da Nang.

Quick summary

  • Da Nang ↔ Hue: train 2h28–3h58 (about 11 trains a day), or car 2–2.5 h via the tunnel, longer if you drive over the Hai Van Pass.
  • Da Nang airport ↔ Hoi An: about 30 km, 45–60 min by car.
  • Tickets: Hue Imperial City 200,000 VND; Hoi An Ancient Town 80,000 or 120,000 VND depending on what it covers.
  • Weather: heavy rain and flood risk from September to November in Hue and Hoi An; February to April is the safest window.

Since 1 July 2025, Quang Nam Province has been merged into Da Nang City, so Hoi An is now part of Da Nang (Resolution 202/2025/QH15). Hue became a centrally governed city on 1 January 2025. You may see old province names on older maps and booking sites.

Which order works best

Most international flights land in Da Nang (DAD). A common loop is Da Nang → Hue → Da Nang → Hoi An, or the reverse. Hue and Hoi An are on opposite sides of Da Nang, so it pays to do Hue as one trip north and Hoi An as one trip south instead of zig-zagging. Hue to Hoi An directly by car takes about 2.5–3.5 hours.

Da Nang airport ↔ Hoi An

  • Distance about 30 km; 45–60 minutes by car.
  • Typical fares seen in 2026: airport taxi 300,000–400,000 VND, ride-hailing 250,000–350,000 VND (secondary source; prices vary with time and traffic).
  • Public bus Route 1: about 30,000 VND, 60–90 minutes, every 20–30 minutes to Hoi An Bus Station.
  • Shuttle vans run from the airport to Da Nang city and Hoi An roughly hourly, about 5:00–23:00.

Hoi An Ancient Town

According to the official Hoi An heritage page (January 2026), you buy one ticket for your stay, valid up to 3 days:

  • 80,000 VND: old-town area + one required site (Japanese Covered Bridge or Quan Công Temple) + 2 sites of your choice.
  • 120,000 VND: old-town area + one required site + one museum + 3 sites of your choice.

Some 2026 listings still describe these as "Vietnamese 80,000 / international 120,000"; the official page describes them by what they cover. Children under 16 are free according to the official page. The ticket also includes free performances, now held at Hoai River Square (01 Cao Hong Lanh St) since March 2026. Walking-street hours are about 9:00–21:30 in summer and 9:00–21:00 in winter.

When to go

  • Hue: wet season September–December, with heavy rain and typhoon risk September–November. Best February–April.
  • Hoi An: rainy September–January, with heavy rain and small floods September–November. Best February–August.
  • Da Nang: rainy season November–February; March–May is good.

If you travel in October or November, keep one flexible day: floods can close the old town riverside and slow the road over the pass.

Sample 5-day plan

  1. Day 1: land in Da Nang, beach afternoon.
  2. Day 2: morning train to Hue along the Hai Van coast; Imperial City in the afternoon.
  3. Day 3: royal tombs (combo ticket), evening car back to Da Nang or straight to Hoi An (2.5–3.5 h).
  4. Day 4: Hoi An Ancient Town, lanterns in the evening.
  5. Day 5: countryside or beach near Hoi An; 45–60 min to the airport.

FAQ

Is there a train to Hoi An?

No railway serves Hoi An; take the train to Da Nang and continue by car, van or bus.

Train or car over the Hai Van Pass?

The train gives coastal views without driving; a car lets you stop at the summit. The tunnel is fastest but skips the views.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport. See Do I need a visa for Vietnam?
